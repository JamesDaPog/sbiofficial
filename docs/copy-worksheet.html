SBI website: copy worksheet

Every piece of text on the site, page by page: 244 slots. The grey “Now” line is what the site says today.

How to use this
  1. Write your text on the lines under each slot. Stay near the length shown.
  2. Write keep to keep the current text. Write cut to remove it (headings and buttons can’t be cut).
  3. Send it back however is easiest: photos of the pages, or typed as H-03: your text, one per line.

Not on this sheet: the application and request form questions (they mirror your Google Forms), the handbook body (your SOP), and page titles for Google. Ask if you want those too.
